What we collect

  • Random device ID – generated on your device and sent to our game server so a dropped game can be resumed and so you are not matched against yourself. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. The server also derives a display name (an animal name and number) from it.
  • Account (optional) – if you sign in, our account provider (Supabase) stores your email address and, when you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and profile picture they share with us. Signing in with Apple lets you hide your real email. The account is used so Pro, purchases and progress follow you to other devices; other players never see it (online you keep an animal name). You can play without an account, and you can delete it at any time in the app.
  • Game data – moves and emoji reactions in a game. A copy of each game in progress is kept on the server so it can continue if the server restarts, and deleted when the game ends (or after 2 hours at most). We do not store game history.
  • Technical data – IP address and connection logs, kept briefly for security and to operate the service.
  • Purchases – handled by Apple/Google and our purchase provider (RevenueCat), which receives an anonymous app-user ID and your entitlement status. We never see your payment details.
  • Advertising – the free version uses [AD NETWORK, e.g. Google AdMob], which may use an advertising identifier and similar data. Personalised ads only with your consent, which you can change at any time in the app's privacy options. On iOS the system's tracking permission applies.
