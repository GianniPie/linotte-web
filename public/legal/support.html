Linotte support

Linotte is a dice and board game for one or two players, offline or online. If something doesn't work, or you have a question, this page tells you how to reach us.

Contact

Write to linotte.cloud@gmail.com. Tell us what happened, which device and system you use, and, if you can, the version shown at the end of How to play in the app. A screenshot helps. We read every message.

Report a problem from the app

Open the menu, go to How to play and tap Report a problem. Write a few words and, if you want, attach a screenshot: the report arrives with your device, app version and the game on screen, so you don't have to describe them.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro window (the PRO button on the home screen) and tap RESTORE PURCHASE. Sign in first with the same account you used to buy it. Purchases stay with your App Store or Google Play account.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: your profile > Delete account. Your account and everything saved with it are deleted for good. Pro bought in the store stays with your store account.

Someone behaved badly or has an offensive name

Tap the player's card and use Report this player. Names reported by several players are removed automatically.

The game disconnected or doesn't find an opponent

Check your connection and try again. If few people are online, a computer-controlled opponent may take the seat, as described in the Terms.
