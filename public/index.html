How levels work
Everyone sees your level. Pro adds a star.
  • Each online game you win, against a random opponent or a friend: +2 points.
  • Each online game you lose or draw, played to the end: +1 point.
  • Every 5 wins against the bot: +1 point.
  • The first online game of the day: +2 bonus points.
  • Watching an ad to the end: +1 point, up to 5 a day.
  • Leaving an online game before the end, or not coming back within a minute after losing the connection: −2 points.
  • Letting 3 turns in a row run out without rolling counts as leaving: −2 points.
  • If your opponent stops playing or disconnects, you can leave at no cost.
  • Up to level 10, each level takes 5 points more than the one before (level 1 at 5 points, 2 at 15, 3 at 30, 10 at 275); after that, every level takes 50 points.
  • Rewards: only the FLAT dice and the green background are free. A new background at levels 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, 16 and 20, new dice at 2, 4, 6, 10 and 15.
  • The badge with your level changes colour as you climb: bronze at 5, silver at 10, gold at 20, ruby at 30 and diamond at 50.
  • Points, and the level with them, can go below zero.
  • Below zero, you only meet other players below zero.
  • Online, the player with more points shows their background to both; each always rolls their own dice.
  • As a guest, your level stays on this phone: an account starts its own.

          How to play

          Line up your pieces on the board: across, down or diagonally. Longer lines score more points.

          Your turn

          1. Roll the five dice. You get up to three rolls.
          2. Between rolls, tap the dice you want to keep. The others are rolled again.
          3. When the dice make a combination, the free tiles for it light up. Tap one to put a piece there.
          4. Tap DONE to end your turn.

          Placing a piece is never required. If three rolls make nothing, your turn simply ends. Each turn lasts 30 seconds.

          Combinations

          • Brelan Three of a kind. It goes on a tile showing that number.
          • Full Three of a kind and a pair.
          • Carré Four of a kind.
          • Petite The five dice add up to 8 or less.
          • Suite 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6.
          • Yam Five of a kind.

          Bonus tiles

          • Sec A full, carré, petite, suite or yam made in one throw of all five dice, without keeping any.
          • Appel After a roll, tap a combination in the strip to call it. If a later roll makes it, you may also use an Appel tile.
            • A brelan can't be called.
            • At least one die must be rolled again.
            • A carré already on the dice can't be called.

          Scoring

          Line of 31 point
          Line of 42 points
          Line of 53 points

          End of the game

          The game ends as soon as a player has placed all 12 pieces or makes a line of five. The highest score wins.
